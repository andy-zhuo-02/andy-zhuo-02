# Hi, I'm Andy Zhuo (卓安) 👋

**Ph.D. Candidate · Peking University · Beijing, China**

I study task planning and control of multi-agent systems at the **School of Advanced Manufacturing and Robotics, Peking University**. Alongside my research, I develop UAV simulation tools and integrate mapping and autonomous navigation for quadruped robots.

## 🔍 Research & Development

- **Research:** Task planning and control of multi-agent systems, with interests in decision-making and motion planning.
- **UAVs:** PX4-based development and simulation with ROS / ROS 2 and Gazebo.
- **Quadruped robots:** SLAM, LiDAR point cloud processing, and autonomous navigation with SLAM Toolbox and Navigation2.
